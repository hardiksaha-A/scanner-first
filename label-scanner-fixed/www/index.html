Tips for a good scan
  • Use bright, even light and avoid glare.
  • Hold steady and fill the frame with the ingredient list.
  • Flatten curved packs. Photograph one panel at a time.
  • Barcodes work best in a photo or camera view with the whole code visible.

How it works

  1. Scan or upload a barcode or the ingredient list.
  2. Check the text we read and fix any mistakes.
  3. Read your result: score, allergy alerts and advice made for you.

Scans can misread text. If you have an allergy, always confirm on the printed label. General guidance only, not medical advice.
